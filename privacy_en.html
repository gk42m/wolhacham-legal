Moonlit Slash — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026
Developer: gk42m (contact seukkang@gmail.com)

This policy explains how "Moonlit Slash" (the "Game") by gk42m (the "Developer") handles personal information.

1. What the Developer collects

The Game has no sign-up or log-in. Your progress and settings are stored only on your device.

When a run is posted to the "Moon Trial" rankings and shadow races (when you set your best for the day), the following is stored in the Developer's Firebase project (Cloud Firestore by Google LLC, Seoul region):

DataPurposeRetention
Anonymous ID (Firebase anonymous authentication — not linked to your name or contacts)Tell your records apartSame as the record
Ranking name (a nickname you choose; "Ninja-number" by default)Shown on the rankingsSame as the record
The run (score, ninja, input log, shadow positions)Rankings, letting other players race your shadow, verifying recordsWhile the service runs

Ranking names, records and shadows are visible to other players. Do not use your real name or contact details as your ranking name.
You can ask for your records to be deleted by email (see contact below).

2. Advertising (third party)

The Game shows rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC), which may process the following to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud.

DataPurposeProcessor
Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID), device and app info (model, OS version, language, app version)Showing and measuring ads, personalized adsGoogle LLC
Approximate location inferred from IP addressRegional ad targeting, fraud preventionGoogle LLC
Ad interactions and error logsMeasurement, qualityGoogle LLC

Google's processing follows the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
This information may be transferred to and processed outside your country (for example in Google data centers in the United States).

3. Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland) and US state privacy laws

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent through Google's consent message (User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the Game's settings ("Privacy & ads"). Without consent, only non-personalized or limited ads are shown.
Where US state privacy laws apply (for example in California), you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for ads through the same settings item.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalization, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

4. Retention and deletion

The Developer keeps no personal information. Game progress stored on your device is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA), and the Developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

6. Your rights

You may delete on-device data at any time by uninstalling the Game. For ad-related data, use the methods in sections 2–3 or contact Google. Under the GDPR you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.

7. Security

Ranking data and ad traffic are encrypted (HTTPS), and access rules let each player write only their own records.
